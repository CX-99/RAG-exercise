# Remote Work Policy
Fictional sample document for this PoC; not a real policy.

## 1. Purpose and eligibility
The company supports a combination of office and remote work where the role can be performed effectively. This fictional policy applies to employees whose duties do not require daily access to a company site. The line manager evaluates eligibility using the role's service commitments, collaboration needs, and ability to protect company information. A remote arrangement is recorded in the team schedule; it does not change contracted working hours, the employee's work location for payroll, or the annual leave entitlement.

Employees should discuss a proposed arrangement with their manager before relying on it for recurring commitments. Managers apply the same job-related criteria across the team and explain declined requests in writing. People Operations reviews concerns about inconsistent treatment. An approved arrangement may be reviewed if duties change, but managers should provide at least 10 working days of notice before a routine change to the schedule. Urgent safety or information-security restrictions may require faster action.

## 2. Schedule and availability
### 2.1 Weekly remote allowance
Eligible employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week with written approval from their line manager. The remaining working days are spent at the assigned office unless annual leave or another approved absence applies.

Employees enter their intended remote days in the shared team calendar by Friday for the following week. Managers can designate a common office day for planning or training, giving at least five working days of notice when practical. Unused remote days do not accumulate and cannot be carried into another week. A remote arrangement is permission to work from an approved location, not permission to take time off; annual leave requests follow the Annual Leave Policy.

### 2.2 Core availability
Employees working remotely must be reachable between 10:00 and 16:00 Singapore time on their scheduled working days, apart from their normal meal break and approved appointments. The line manager agrees how the remaining contracted hours are scheduled.

Reachable means checking the team's agreed communication channels and being able to join planned meetings; it does not require uninterrupted video monitoring. Employees update their status when temporarily unavailable and tell the meeting organiser if an appointment conflicts with a meeting. Teams should avoid assuming that remote staff will respond outside agreed hours. An urgent operational rota, if required for a role, must be communicated separately by the manager.

## 3. Workspace and equipment
### 3.1 Workspace assessment
Before beginning regular remote work, employees complete the workspace checklist and obtain their line manager's approval. The checklist covers a stable work surface, an adjustable chair, adequate lighting, and a private place for confidential conversations.

Employees report material changes to the workspace so the manager can reconsider its suitability. A shared household is not automatically disqualifying, but company papers and screens must be protected from visitors and other occupants. Employees should take normal breaks and report work-related discomfort promptly. People Operations can arrange a workspace discussion without requesting photographs of unrelated private areas of the home.

### 3.2 Equipment responsibilities
The company supplies one managed laptop for approved remote work; IT retains ownership and manages its configuration. Employees must use that laptop for company information and may not share it with household members.

A suitable desk, chair, and peripherals remain the employee's responsibility, subject to the reimbursement rules in the Travel and Expenses Policy. Workspace approval does not authorise a purchase or promise reimbursement. Employees obtain the separate advance purchase approval required by Finance and check the remaining allowance before ordering equipment. IT should assess compatibility before an employee purchases an accessory that connects to the managed laptop. Company equipment must be returned when IT requests it or employment ends.

## 4. Connectivity and location
Employees maintain a reliable connection suitable for scheduled calls and document access. If a connection fails for more than one hour, notify the line manager using another available channel and agree whether to work from the office. Avoid confidential work in public areas where other people can see the screen or hear discussions. Access to company systems follows the Information Security Policy, including approved remote access and incident reporting.

The ordinary arrangement covers the employee's recorded home address in Singapore. Working from another country requires written review by People Operations and Information Security before travel is booked. Approval is not automatic and must specify the dates and permitted access. Employees should never treat a business travel approval or a holiday booking as approval to relocate their normal working location. Managers review remote arrangements every six months with the employee.
